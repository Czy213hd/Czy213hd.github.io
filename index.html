About Me

Hello! I am Zhongyu Chen, a junior undergraduate in mechanical engineering at Zhejiang University and the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. I am also a robotics researcher at the Physical Intelligence Lab under the supervision of Prof. Hua Chen.
