About Me

Hello! I am Zhongyu Chen, a junior undergraduate at Zhejiang University and the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. I am also a robotics researcher at the Physical Intelligence Lab under the supervision of Prof. Hua Chen.

My research focuses on robotics, especially locomotion, manipulation, and how to deploy robots in real-world environments.

My ultimate goal is to become a full-stack engineer who can help robots perform more real-world activities and become more intelligent. I am open to communication and collaboration on robotics, machine learning, and engineering projects.
